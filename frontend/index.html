Under the hood

ResUNet1D, built for spectra

Spectrapur is powered by a 1D Residual U-Net purpose-built for spectral denoising — engineered to avoid the peak shifting and peak rounding that generic image-style denoisers tend to introduce.

  • Multi-scale dilated context blocks capture both local peak shape and broader baseline structure.
  • Attention-gated skip connections and squeeze-excite blocks calibrate which features pass through at each resolution.
  • A spectral-attention module gives extra weight to the fingerprint region, where peak identity matters most.
  • Trained with a composite loss covering peak alignment, curvature, slope, FFT topology, and amplitude fidelity — not just MSE.
